Welcome to peapickers. My name is Jonas, the creator of this website.

Let me start by explaining why I chose peapickers as the catchphrase for this platform.

It's a reference to the title ‘Destitute Peapickers’, which is a set of pictures shot by Dorothea Lange in the 1930s. To cite Wikipedia 'it is a derogatory reference to poor, migrant workers during the Great Depression. These people were unskilled, poorly educated workers, employable only in menial jobs. Receiving poor wages for working long hours under dreadful conditions' (Wikipedia).

While it's a little out there to compare 1930s working conditions of migrants during the Great Depression to my life, it made me think about the many people today who might feel the same. Stuck in some menial jobs which does not reflect your thoughts or creativity.

During my years at art school, I had the pleasure to watch mind-blowing artworks, most of which never saw the outside of school. Their attraction lay in their rawness. What I felt was authenticity, unfiltered emotions and thoughts. Formal schooling in art and the influence it has on it's value feels extremely trivial. Everyone creates art that can be felt.

I believe ALL authentic artworks deserves a place to be seen on -at least- this digital platform.

Authentic means that you have bared your emotion, intellect, soul, realness inside the physical form of an artwork.

WHAT CAN I OFFER WITH THIS PLATFORM

  • REPRODUCTIONS of your authentic artworks: I have the tools needed to reproduce your physical artworks into digital files. I can deliver you these files and place them on this platform.
  • PRINTING of authentic artworks: I can print your digital files on high quality paper with a minimum size of A7 and a maximum size of A2. I will try my best to make placing orders a thing on the website.
  • MONTLY EXPOSITIONS of active artists. Possible if the platform is large or known enough. I will fight my social anxiety if this becomes a reality.
  • PUBLIC ARCHIVE of authentic artworks: Small and forever incomplete, but at least it's something.

MY ECONOMIC MODEL

I don't have it all worked out yet. There is not yet a fiscal structure in which I can receive or deposit money. I am considering a vzw or a bv.

What I'm planning to do:

  • Transparency is key; An excel-sheet where everyone can what is sold with a date and pricetag attached. Who bought this will not be visible. This kind of automation might be hard to construct, so I'm not sure if this is possible.
  • You sold something through the website? Consumer Price minus Production cost minus 15%fee = Your Profit. In other words, excluding the production costs, 18 of the 20 peas are for the creator. Arranging other payment-structures like 'pay what you can' in the same way seems predictable and fair for everyone.
  • Quality Control: I don't know how to keep the quality of the artworks on the platform as authentic as possible. The word control implies that a central authority screens all artworks and makes a selection. This would guard the feel of authenticity and realness of artworks that are placed on this platform. The art must be felt, technique is not a defining quality. But adding this idea comes with a sense of entitlement. Who and how can someone -with the ugliest word- enforce such an idea? This feels wrong, though it a baselayer of this might be necessary in some way.. Something I'll have to think about.
— Jonas, peapickers
