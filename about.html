Peapickers refers to the title ‘Destitute Peapickers’, which is a set of pictures shot by Dorothea Lange in the 1930s. To cite Wikipedia: “it is a derogatory reference to poor, migrant workers during the Great Depression. These people were unskilled, poorly educated workers, employable only in menial jobs. Receiving poor wages for working long hours under dreadful conditions” (Wikipedia).

While the current welfare state with good working conditions and decent wages differ greatly from the 1930s working conditions of migrants during the Great Depression, its sentiment holds. For me it means the feeling of being stuck in a continuous series of menial jobs that will never reflect the intellect or creativity of what economic society deems to be the non‑skilled. Modern menial jobs are executed by those unable or unwilling to monetize their version of reality.

This platform is named in solidarity with everyone who creates despite being told their work doesn’t count.
