Welcome to peapickers. My name is Jonas, the creator of this website.

Let me start by explaining why I chose peapickers as the catchphrase for this platform.

It's a reference to the title ‘Destitute Peapickers’, which is a set of pictures shot by Dorothea Lange in the 1930s. To cite Wikipedia 'it is a derogatory reference to poor, migrant workers during the Great Depression. These people were unskilled, poorly educated workers, employable only in menial jobs. Receiving poor wages for working long hours under dreadful conditions' (Wikipedia).

While it's a little out there to compare 1930s working conditions of migrants during the Great Depression to my life, it made me think about the many people today who might feel the same. Stuck in some menial jobs which does not reflect your thoughts or creativity.

During my years at art school, I had the pleasure to watch mind-blowing artworks, most of which never saw the outside of school. Their attraction lay in their rawness. What I felt was authenticity, unfiltered emotions and thoughts. Formal schooling in art and the influence it has on it's real value is at the most trivial. Everyone creates art that can be felt.

This platform is that place for raw authenticity.

With raw authenticity I mean soulful, unmanufactured, a true investment of undiluted visceral emotion and intellect. Personal meaning is essential.

WHAT CAN I OFFER WITH THIS PLATFORM

  • VISIBILITY: Connecting your artworks with your digital identity on this platform can make you reach a broader audience. If you do not want this, anonimity can be provided.
  • EXPOSITIONS: Possible if the platform is large or known enough. I will fight my social anxiety if this becomes a reality.
  • REPRODUCTIONS: I have the tools needed to reproduce your physical artworks into digital files. I can deliver you these files and place them on this platform.
  • HIGH QUALITY PRINTS: I can print your digital files on high quality paper with a minimum size of A7 and a maximum size of A2. I will try my best to make placing orders a thing on the website.
  • PUBLIC ARCHIVE: Small and forever incomplete, but real.
  • QUALITY: Every artist can show a limt of six artworks. This treshold is meant to keep the raw quality high. An physical exposition is the place to show more.

ECONOMIC MODEL

I don't have it all worked out yet. There is not yet a fiscal structure in which I can receive or deposit money. I am considering a vzw (easy to work with) or a bv (easy to grow).

What I'm planning to do:

  • Transparency is key. Your images stay your own, are not scraped by AI on the platform and are undownloadable by visitors. These guardrails will be implemented before the first contemporary artist enters the platform.
  • You sold something through the website? Consumer Price minus Production cost minus 15%fee = Your Profit. Payment-structures like 'pay what you can' or a set price can work in the same way. This sounds predictable and fair for everyone.
