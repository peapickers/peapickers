Welcome to peapickers.vzw — a digital platform for raw, authentic art.

With raw authentic art I mean soulful and unmanufactured, a true investment of undiluted visceral emotion and intellect.

WHAT CAN I OFFER WITH THIS PLATFORM

  • VISIBILITY: Connecting your artworks or portfolio on this platform can make you reach a broader audience. Pseudonymity can be provided.
  • EXPOSITIONS: Monthly physical expos in Ghent or Brussels are a final goal, once the platform has grown.
  • REPRODUCTIONS: I can digitise your physical works and place them here. Contact me to discuss.
  • HIGH QUALITY PRINTS: Sell your originals or high‑quality prints. I print on demand and ship directly.
  • PUBLIC ARCHIVE: Small and forever incomplete, but real.
  • QUALITY: Raw Authenticity is the baselayer. Please do not provide glossified products made for an audience — show what you feel is your reality.
  • SAFETY: Your images remain yours. They will be licensed, undownloadable, and protected from AI scraping while on this platform. These guardrails will be in place before the first contemporary artist joins.
