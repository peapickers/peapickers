Welcome to peapickers. My name is Jonas, the creator of this website.

Let me start by explaining why I chose peapickers as the catchphrase for this platform.

It refers to the title ‘Destitute Peapickers’, which is a set of pictures shot by Dorothea Lange in the 1930s. To cite Wikipedia 'it is a derogatory reference to poor, migrant workers during the Great Depression. These people were unskilled, poorly educated workers, employable only in menial jobs. Receiving poor wages for working long hours under dreadful conditions' (Wikipedia).

While the current normalisation of polycrisis feels different from the 1930s working conditions of migrants during the Great Depression, it's sentiment holds. For me it means the feeling of being stuck in a continuous series of menial jobs whom will never reflect the intellect or creativity of what society deems to be the non-skilled. These economic functions are executed by those unable or unwilling to monetize their version of reality.

I want this platform to be a public accessible digital exposition place where these unfiltered emotions and thoughts can exist.

Your version of raw authenticity must not be kept on a forgotten hard drive or dusty portfolio in some closet.

With raw authentic art I mean soulful and unmanufactured, a true investment of undiluted visceral emotion and intellect.

WHAT CAN I OFFER WITH THIS PLATFORM

  • VISIBILITY: Connecting your artworks or portfolio on this platform can make you reach a broader audience. If you feel private about your art, but still want an audience, pseudonymity can be provided.
  • EXPOSITIONS: Possible if the platform is large or known enough. Physical expos in Ghent or Brussels is a goal.
  • REPRODUCTIONS: I have the tools needed to reproduce your physical artworks into digital files. Contact me and we can create digital files of your physical work and place them on this platform.
  • HIGH QUALITY PRINTS: Selling your original artwork or copies of your original work is choice you can make. With your permission, I can print your digital files on high quality paper as a postcard or poster and send it to the buyer.
  • PUBLIC ARCHIVE: Small and forever incomplete, but real.
  • QUALITY: Raw Authenticity is the baselayer of this platform. Please do not provide products for an audience, only provide what you feel to be your reality of raw authenticity
  • SAFETY: Your images will always stay your images. They must be licensed, will be undownloadable and will not be scraped by AI. These guardrails will be implemented before the first contemporary artist enters the platform.
