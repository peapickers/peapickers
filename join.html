We welcome artists whose primary income is not from their art — those who create after their day jobs, in the margins, for love rather than profit.

The three‑point check

  1. No AI‑generated or AI‑assisted work.
  2. No purely commercial or stock‑like aesthetics. If it could be a banner ad, it isn’t for here.
  3. You identify as a “peapicker” — someone whose main income is a day job, not their art.

This isn’t a jury. It’s a conversation. If your work doesn’t fit, I’ll tell you why and suggest where it might.

Ready to submit? Send an email to jonas.vervaet@proton.me with 3‑5 images and a few words about your practice. Or use the form (coming soon).
