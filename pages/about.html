The Name

Peapickers refers to the title ‘Destitute Peapickers’, a set of pictures shot by Dorothea Lange in the 1930s. Wikipedia describes it as a derogatory reference to poor, migrant workers during the Great Depression — people employable only in menial jobs, working long hours for miserable wages. While today’s working conditions in Europe are vastly different, the emotional echo remains: the feeling of being stuck in a cycle of jobs that ignore your intellect and creativity. This platform is named in solidarity with everyone who makes art despite being told their work doesn’t count.

What Peapickers Is

Peapickers.vzw is a pure online archive — a digital safe harbour for artworks that exist because they had to. It’s a print‑on‑demand webshop that sells high‑quality posters. In the future we aim to coordinate with local small businesses for framing, bookbinding, and magazine printing, always at clear, defineable low prices. Every order from the public‑domain collection directly funds the non‑profit platform. Contemporary artists receive the lion’s share of each sale, with a transparent 5–15% platform fee.

Our Stance on AI

We actively block known AI crawlers and implement technical deterrents (low‑resolution previews, disabled right‑click, referrer checks). Read our full safety guidelines for an honest assessment of what we can — and cannot — protect against.

Safety & Pseudonymity

Complete protection is impossible. Screenshots, camera photos, and determined scraping can happen. What we offer is a set of honest measures and the strong recommendation to use pseudonymity. Your real name can stay disconnected from your most personal work if so desired. We publish clear safety guidelines, never promise absolute security, and encourage you to decide what level of exposure you’re comfortable with.

How Money Works

Sales flow through the non‑profit. For contemporary art, the calculation is simple: Retail Price – taxes – Platform Fee (flat 10-20%) = Artist’s Share. We keep that fee as low as possible to sustain the vzw’s bare operations. All public‑domain poster revenue, after production costs, stays inside the platform.

Artists are paid via royalty or compensation agreements, with full transparency. We can provide yearly statements and, eventually, a real‑time dashboard. If you’re unsure about the fiscal side in Belgium, we’ll walk you through it — no one should miss out because of administrative fear.

The Peapickers Ethos

We are not a mass marketplace. Every artist is known to us personally; there is always at least one direct human contact between the vzw and the artist. No automated mass onboarding. This keeps the ecosystem small, trusted, and unglossy.
